## 1. Problem & method

Bridgewater's All-Weather portfolio is risk-parity-ish but has a known weak
spot: when **equities AND bonds fall together** — 2022, Q4'18, parts of
2008, the 1994 / 2013 / 2021 bond routs — the portfolio suffers because its
two big risk sleeves are long equities + long duration. This system searches
for a long-only, no-leverage, ETF-investable **Equal-Risk-Contribution** risk-
parity portfolio that is more resilient to that both-down scenario.

- **Combination unit:** asset-class buckets (curated distinct sleeves).
- **Backtest window:** 2008-01-31 → 2026-07-31 (223 months).
- **Risk parity:** long-only ERC (Maillard-Roncalli-Teiletche), solved by
  cyclical coordinate descent (pure NumPy, no scipy). Monthly rebalance to
  ERC target weights; broad search refit cadence = `A`; winner &
  benchmark re-run at monthly refit. Trailing covariance = 36m.
- **Stress test:** (a) conditional *both-down* months — months where the
  broad equity reference AND broad bond reference are both negative
  (53 such months in window: 2008-02-29, 2008-06-30, 2008-09-30, 2008-10-31, 2009-01-31, 2009-02-28, 2009-10-31, 2010-05-31, 2010-11-30, 2011-09-30, 2011-11-30, 2012-05-31, 2013-02-28, 2013-05-31, 2013-06-30, 2013-08-31, 2013-11-30, 2014-07-31, 2014-09-30, 2014-12-31, 2015-06-30, 2015-08-31, 2015-11-30, 2015-12-31, 2016-05-31, 2016-10-31, 2016-11-30, 2018-02-28, 2018-08-31, 2018-10-31, 2020-03-31, 2020-09-30, 2021-01-31, 2021-09-30, 2021-11-30, 2022-01-31, 2022-02-28, 2022-04-30, 2022-06-30, 2022-08-31, 2022-09-30, 2022-12-31, 2023-02-28, 2023-05-31, 2023-08-31, 2023-09-30, 2023-10-31, 2024-01-31, 2024-04-30, 2024-10-31, 2024-12-31, 2025-03-31, 2026-03-31); plus (b) named
  historical crisis windows (see §4).
- **Sleeves searched (12):** US Equity, International Equity, US REIT, Preferred Stock, US Treasuries, US Corporate Bonds, US Municipal Bonds, EM Bonds, Gold, Silver, Commodities, Currency.
- **Combinations backtested:** 3301 (every subset with ≥1 equity
  and ≥1 bond sleeve, 2–8 sleeves).
- **Resilience score:** 100 × (0.40·pct(Sharpe) + 0.40·pct(both-down ann.
  return) + 0.20·pct(−max drawdown)). Higher = better.

## 6. Caveats & limitations

- **Lookahead / refit:** ERC target weights are re-solved from a trailing
  36-month covariance using only data prior to the rebalance
  date (no lookahead). The first ~12 months of the window use equal-weight
  until enough history accumulates.
- **Volatility sleeve:** represented by ^VIX-based returns in the data. A
  long-vol position is *not* cheaply buy-and-hold-able (VXX carries a heavy
  roll cost). Treat any winner that loads on Volatility as illustrative of
  the *crisis-alpha / tail-hedge* role, not a literal ETF hold. Re-run with
  `--exclude-volatility` for a strictly buy-and-hold-able universe.
- **Digital assets** only begin 2015; run `--include-digital` for a modern-
  era pass that adds BTC-style exposure (shorter window, different regime).
- **Returns, not total-return accounting for the sleeve aggregates:** the
  monthly asset-class series are Yahoo-derived; bond sleeves include
  distributions where Yahoo provides them but see `docs/nuances_and_caveats.md`.
- **Sharpe uses rf=0** for cross-portfolio comparability; absolute levels are
  optimistic but rankings are unaffected.
- **Collinear sleeves removed** from the search universe (World Equity,
  Gold/Precious Metals, US Bonds aggregate) to keep ERC well-conditioned;
  they remain in the data file and are used in the stress references.
- **No leverage, long-only:** fully invested (weights sum to 1). Achieved
  portfolio volatility is whatever ERC produces; do not expect a fixed vol.
- **One regime, one window:** resilience is measured in-sample over
  2008-01-31–2026-07-31. Out-of-sample stability should be
  checked with a rolling walk-forward before any real use.

## 7. Reproduce

```bash
.venv/bin/python risk_parity_backtest.py          # defaults (this report)
.venv/bin/python risk_parity_backtest.py --refit M   # monthly refit (slower)
.venv/bin/python risk_parity_backtest.py --exclude-volatility   # no VXX-style
.venv/bin/python risk_parity_backtest.py --include-digital      # + crypto pass
```

Outputs in `output/risk_parity/`: `combinations_results.csv`,
`top_portfolios.csv`, `winning_portfolio.csv`, `benchmark_allweather.csv`,
`stress_drilldown.csv`, `equity_curves.csv`, `report.md`.
